# Illustrated worlds — proposal 01

The three PNGs here are new original image-generation outputs for the owner's requested anime-background direction. They are **design assets**, not installed wallpapers or photographs. `PROMPTS.json` records the complete prompt set and built-in tool mode; `PROVENANCE.json` records hashes, dimensions and licensing. No reference screenshots, character images or third-party recordings were supplied.

## Shared art language

Hand-painted 2D environmental backgrounds: designed silhouettes, expressive coloured light, readable drawn architecture and grouped foliage/shadow masses. Keep worlds atmospheric and distinct while using a coherent composition/colour discipline. The intended direction is illustrated animation scenery, not photographic texture with a filter. These are first proposals for visual review; the owner has not approved them as final art.

Scenes carry no UI, product words, numbers, worldline labels, character portraits or legal banners. Desktop controls must remain in the shell. Leave usable sky and quieter central regions, with stronger environmental detail at the lower third/edges. The drawings are atmospheric enough to view alone but should recede behind opaque application windows.

## Crops, resolution and practical use

Generated native size is **1672×941** for each scene. The boards scale/crop them to display targets; that does not make them native 1080p or 4K masters. Final release art needs higher-resolution source production and a fresh crop/quality review.

- 16:9: retain the full horizon/sky balance. Keep the laboratory window, shrine edge or island lane as a quiet side anchor.
- 16:10: crop a small amount from left/right. Maintain the horizon and avoid losing the primary scene anchor.
- Ultrawide: commission a wider composition rather than stretch the image. Plan multi-monitor placement with independent crops and a neutral fallback.
- Large text/high contrast: applications use opaque backing surfaces; art brightness cannot determine text colour. Offer the same shell on a plain graphite background.
- Never stretch the scene non-uniformly or bake current controls into it. A panel's clear area is a composition consideration, not a permanent strip in the image.

Production review should inspect cloud/roof line continuity, small repetitive structures, illegible pseudo-signs, gradients/banding, display crops and behaviour behind ordinary apps. The generated scenes include no intentional text, but final distribution assets still need visual approval at release resolution.
